# Level 01 — The Valley of the Lost Egg

Target playtime: 8–12 minutes on a first successful run.

## 0–20% — Fern Gate
Movement onboarding under light pressure. Dense jungle foreground, distant sauropod silhouettes, first small ground enemy. A high optional route teaches the player to look above the obvious path.

## 20–45% — Broken Nest
The player discovers the empty nest and tracks. Ancient snakes introduce timing-based hazards. First checkpoint follows the discovery so the narrative beat is not repeatedly interrupted.

## 45–70% — Echo Cave
Tighter platforming, falling rock telegraphs and short dark chambers. Two-route design: safer lower path and difficult upper secret path. Secret 1 contains a fossil chamber.

## 70–90% — Skybreak
The cave opens onto cliffs. Flying enemies patrol horizontally before later variants dive. Moving platforms cross a ravine. Checkpoint 2 precedes the final set-piece.

## 90–100% — Lava Vista
Short chase-like sequence with crumbling footing. Across the ravine the Palaszarusz appears with the egg. It roars, causes a rockfall and disappears toward the volcano. Exit opens toward Level 02.

## Secrets
1. Fossil chamber behind a risky upper cave route.
2. Hidden canopy route reached by chaining moving platforms near Skybreak.

## Fairness rules
- No enemy spawns directly on the player.
- First instance of every hazard is isolated and readable.
- Checkpoints precede the cave escalation and finale.
- Flying dive attacks require a visible/audio tell.
