# Story — The Lost Egg

A strange aurora opens a rift above the familiar world. On the other side lies a prehistoric valley untouched by time. Soon after arrival, the heroes discover a freshly abandoned nest and a single trail leading toward the volcano.

The egg has been taken.

Following the trail reveals that the valley is being disturbed by the raging Palaszarusz. Its movements drive animals out of their habitats and destabilize caves and cliffs. The first chapter ends when the creature appears across a lava ravine carrying the stolen egg deeper toward the crater.

The adventure is not about hunting dinosaurs: many creatures are neutral wildlife. The player must learn which animals are threats, which are environmental hazards, and which can indirectly help open routes.
