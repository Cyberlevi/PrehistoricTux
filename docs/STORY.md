# Story

Tux discovers a shattered nest and one missing egg. The trail cuts through the mountains, then underground, then into a forgotten region where dinosaurs have survived in isolation.

The raptors are territorial hunters. Pterosaurs attack from exposed cliffs. Triceratops-like guardians protect old routes and nesting grounds. As the journey continues, the player learns that the dinosaurs are not a single enemy faction.

At the volcanic fortress, Tux reaches Palaszarusz. The creature has carried the egg to a protected nesting site rather than destroying it. The final confrontation is framed as a misunderstanding resolved only after surviving the crater and reaching the egg.
